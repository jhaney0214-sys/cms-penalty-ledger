Where a home has changed owner since 2016, CMS's change-of-ownership file (edition 2026-06-01) gives the date its current owner took it over, and penalties from before that date are counted apart: they were against an earlier owner, not a penalty against this chain's management. These are counts, not rates. They are not adjusted for how many homes the chain has, how large they are, or which states inspect them.
