Where a home has changed owner since 2016, CMS's change-of-ownership file (edition 2026-06-01) gives the date its current owner took it over, and penalties from before that date are counted apart: they were against an earlier owner, not a penalty against this chain's management. The figure can be off both ways: a change between two companies in the same chain is counted the same way, and a change the file does not hold (it runs to changes effective by 2026-02-01) is not counted at all.

16 of the 48 homes changed owner since 2016. 16 of those penalties, $286,108 in fines, are dated before the home's current owner took it over, which leaves $1,925,352 in fines since.

These are counts, not rates. They are not adjusted for how many homes the chain has, how large they are, or which states inspect them.
