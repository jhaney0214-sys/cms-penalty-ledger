Download the data

Everything the pages on this site show comes from two files. Both are here whole, with what each column holds. They are built from the monthly editions the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services publishes and then archives; CMS's current file keeps about three years, and these keep every edition back to January 2019.

Every penalty in CMS's archive

One row for each penalty CMS has published against a nursing home, with the first and last edition that listed it and whether CMS still does.

How to cite it

Nursing Home Penalty Ledger, penalty history through CMS's file of 2026-10-08, https://penalty-ledger.pages.dev/data. Built from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services' Provider Data Catalog and its archive of monthly editions (data.cms.gov).

Give the date: the files grow each month, and a count quoted without one cannot be checked. For a single home, cite its page, which links each penalty to the CMS edition that carried it.

What it is, and what it is not

  • The facts are CMS's public record. This site arranges them and adds nothing: every row can be found in the CMS edition named by its first_seen date.
  • A penalty CMS no longer publishes was not withdrawn or overturned by that. CMS's file is a rolling window, and a row leaves it by age.
  • An amount can change while CMS lists it. amount is the last one published and first_amount the first; a source quoting either may be right.
  • Fines and payment denials are different penalties and are never added together here.
  • A star rating moves when a home changes and also when CMS changes its method. The file cannot tell the two apart.
  • In CMS's edition of 2019-10-09, 101 home numbers were stored as numbers (14E169 as 1.40E+170). Each is restored here where exactly one home it could be is known from the editions on either side.
